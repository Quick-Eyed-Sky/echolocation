# Echolocation 1.0.0

The first public version.

- Five cards read bottom to top in three tiers: State (Stance, Form), Movement (Move, Twist), Proposition (Constraint), with a Strategy sentence as the image.
- Moving cards turn into another card from the same deck; 32 named figures.
- The "I'm stuck" joker, with five situations.
- 235 cards and 32 figures, all written by hand in one notebook (2004–2011). Nothing is generated.
- One static page, no packages, no tracking. Light and dark themes.
